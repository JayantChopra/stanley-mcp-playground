# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This cookbook exists so people can learn to run their content operation through Stanley and Claude. We want the project to be a place where anyone can ask a beginner question, share a recipe, or critique a workflow without meeting hostility.

Everyone who participates is expected to keep discussions constructive and welcoming across experience levels, backgrounds, and identities.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience, especially newcomers working through their first connection.
- Critique recipes, prompts, and workflows without attacking the person who wrote them.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, and ask a clarifying question before escalating a disagreement.
- Accept feedback and revise when the evidence changes.
- Give credit for recipes, ideas, and reports that came from others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Insults, slurs, demeaning language, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing someone's private information without clear permission. This includes screenshots of another person's analytics, calendar, or drafts shared without their consent.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeated bad-faith argument after being asked to stop.
- Spam, self-promotion disguised as recipes, or abuse of project infrastructure.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report violations privately by emailing [jay.chopra@stanwith.me](mailto:jay.chopra@stanwith.me), through [GitHub's report abuse flow](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse), or by contacting the maintainer, [@JayantChopra](https://github.com/JayantChopra), directly. Include what context you are comfortable sharing: links, screenshots, dates. Reports are handled with discretion and shared only with people who need them to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider proportionate: a request to change behavior, removal of comments or contributions, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Decisions weigh the impact of the behavior, the risk of continued harm, and prior conduct.
