# 06 · Repurposing

**What you get:** one post you already wrote, redrafted for every platform you're on, with quote cards and carousels staged as media.

**Needs:** X connected as the usual source, plus whichever targets you want to hit (LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram, Substack Notes); run /stanley-start if you are not sure what you have.

Good content dies in one feed. A thread that worked on X can carry a LinkedIn post and an Instagram carousel without you writing anything new, so Stanley does the conversion and you approve the results.

## Pick a source

Stanley starts from any of these:

- a URL to one of your X threads or posts
- a LinkedIn post, linked or pasted in
- "my best post this month", which pulls the top performer from analytics the way [recipe 04](04-analytics-review.md) does

You don't need to copy anything over. Hand him the link or the description and he fetches the rest.

## Core walkthrough

1. Name the source and the targets in one message.

   > Take this thread and repurpose it: [URL]. I want a LinkedIn post, a Threads chain, and an Instagram carousel.

2. Stanley replies with one draft per platform, each wrapped in a tag like `<linkedin_post>` or `<threads_post>`. Read them as standalone posts, not translations; the LinkedIn version has to work for someone who never saw the thread. Ask for changes the way you'd ask a person, and if you edit a draft yourself, paste it back with the wrapper tag intact so Stanley knows which platform's rules to check it against.

   > The LinkedIn draft buries the lead. Open with the result from tweet 4 and cut the hashtags.

3. For a quote card, point at your strongest single post. Stanley screenshots the X post into an image and stages it as media you can attach to a draft.

   > Turn this post into a quote-card image: [URL]. Attach it to the LinkedIn draft.

4. For a carousel, he renders the thread into slides. Tell him how to split it, or let him follow the thread's own beats.

   > Build a carousel from that thread, one tweet per slide, and save it for Instagram.

5. Approve what you like and tell Stanley where to put it.

   > Schedule the LinkedIn post and the Threads chain into my next open slots. Hold the Instagram carousel as a draft.

## Where things land

Text drafts go onto your calendar as scheduled drafts, which you can move or delete later ([recipe 02](02-content-calendar.md) covers the calendar). Quote cards and carousel slides are staged media, attached to whichever drafts you named. Instagram is the exception: Stanley prepares the carousel and caption, but nothing on Instagram ever auto-publishes, so the finished slides wait in your photo gallery until you post them yourself.

Character limits, file sizes, and per-platform attachment rules live in [recipe 09](09-what-stanley-can-do.md). Stanley enforces them while drafting, so a thread too long for a single Threads post comes back as a chain instead of an error.

## Going the other way

The same pattern runs in reverse. Suppose a LinkedIn post of yours landed and you want it on X:

> This LinkedIn post did well: [URL]. Cut it down to a single X post, and give me a thread version too so I can compare.

## When it goes wrong

**Stanley can't read the source URL.** He reads public posts; a link behind a login wall stays closed to him, so paste the text in instead.

**Drafts for one platform land nowhere or error out.** The connection has probably expired. [Recipe 01](01-first-connection.md) covers reconnecting.

**The converted draft sounds generic.** Name the lines to keep verbatim; Stanley carries them across unchanged.

**The carousel splits in odd places.** Give him the boundaries yourself: say slide 1 is the hook, slides 2 to 5 are one tip each, and the last slide is the ask.
